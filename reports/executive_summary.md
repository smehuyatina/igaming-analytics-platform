# Synthetic portfolio findings

Independent simulation; no real company or employment experience.

- Observation: January 2024–December 2025 (24 complete months).
- Observed GGR: KRW 6,120,352,862; bonus-only NGR: KRW 5,877,924,012.
- Highest total NGR signup channel: organic (KRW 2,565,771,531). This is an association shaped by generator assumptions, not incremental return.
- Exact D1 registration retention: 20.19% (4,433/21,958 eligible users).
- Exact D7 registration retention: 17.94% (3,899/21,728 eligible users).
- Exact D30 registration retention: 11.15% (2,319/20,794 eligible users).

## Suggested next analysis

Compare channels with matched 90-day cohorts (query 18), then design a randomized measurement plan before changing budget. Review payment failures by provider only if such a field is collected. Investigate retention differences after adjusting for tenure; the simulation does not establish causality.

## Limits

No real market benchmarks, wallet accounting, fraud labels, taxes, payment fees or bonus redemption. The NGR model is deliberately simplified; 90-day LTV is an observed proxy. Account status is an end-of-window snapshot.
